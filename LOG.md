- `create-next-app` 16.4 turns on Cache Components; the first `/app` read cookies outside `<Suspense>` and the build failed. Fixed with a Suspense boundary and a Skeleton.
- Log out "didn't say anything": the toast showed for about 4 s while the page changed, so it was easy to miss. Added a lasting "You're logged out" notice on the login page.
- Next 16 keeps visited pages alive in the background, so after log-out the old login form came back with the typed email and password and a stuck "Logging in..." button. Fixed: log-in clears the form when it succeeds, and log-out does a full page load to `/login?loggedOut=1`, so nothing from the session stays in memory.
- A 404 for `/app?_rsc=...` showed in the browser console after every login (my first note blamed the Back button; that was wrong). Cause: right after login, Next prefetched the "Create an account" link on the hidden login page; `proxy.ts` redirected that background request (logged-in user asking for `/signup`), and Next followed the redirect without its RSC header and got 404. Next strips its prefetch headers before `proxy.ts` runs, so the proxy can't tell prefetches apart. Fixed with `prefetch={false}` on the links between `/login` and `/signup`. Rule for later steps: links between logged-in and logged-out pages don't prefetch.
- After the full-page log-out, the "You're logged out" toast no longer showed (a page load clears toasts). The login page now fires the toast itself from `?loggedOut=1`, next to the notice in the card.
